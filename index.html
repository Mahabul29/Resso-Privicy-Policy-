Resso Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Resso Android app ("Resso", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile and activity you create. Your display name, username, profile photo link, the songs you like, the artists you follow, and comments you post on songs. These are stored in Google Firestore. Comments are shown to other users together with your display name.
  • Data stored on your device. Listening history, playback state, equalizer and language settings are saved locally on your phone.
  • Searches and playback. Song and artist searches, and requests to play music, are sent to a third-party music data service (JioSaavn) so the app can show results and stream audio.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads from Monetag. The ad provider may collect device and browser identifiers, IP address and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads.
  • Announcements. The app contacts our server from time to time to check for new announcements. Like any server, it may receive your IP address in its logs.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet to load music, sign in and sync your data.
  • Notifications to show playback controls and announcements.
  • Foreground service (media playback) to keep music playing in the background.
  • Modify audio settings for the equalizer.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, microphone, camera, SMS or precise location.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, saving likes and follows, comments, playback.
  • To show ads that help keep the app free.
  • To send announcements, keep the service secure, and fix problems.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the services the app relies on: Google (Firebase Authentication and Firestore), the JioSaavn music data service, and Monetag for advertising. Each handles data under its own privacy policy. Comments you post are visible to other users of the app.

5. Retention and deletion

Account, profile, likes, follows and comments are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete them. You can remove your own comments in the app. To delete your account and associated data, email us at the address below and we will remove it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything stored on your device.

6. Children

Resso is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Google Firebase security rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and continued use of the app means you accept the update.
